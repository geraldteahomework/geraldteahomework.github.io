Language

Subjects

The subjects you can pick when adding homework. Each one has a colour for the calendar and its labels. Classes from your timetable are added automatically.

Timetable

Your classes for each day. One class per line, optionally with a start time, e.g. 08:00 Math. With times, the app knows which class you're in right now.

Week A

Week B

Holidays

School breaks. Repeating homework skips them, the A/B week counter pauses, and the calendar marks them.

Terms

Terms or semesters. The checklist counts down the school days left, grade averages and stats can be viewed per term, and completed work from earlier terms is tucked away. To start a new term, add one that begins today.

Grading scale

How grades are written at your school. Changing this doesn't convert grades already entered.

Repeating homework

Things that come back every week (or two). Upcoming ones are created automatically; add a new one from the checklist by choosing "Every week" when you add it.

Appearance

Phone notifications

Reminders are sent through ntfy, a free notification app. Install ntfy on your phone, subscribe to a topic name of your choosing (make it hard to guess), and enter the same name below.

When to remind me

Days before the due date to get a reminder (comma-separated, e.g. 7, 3, 1). Items with a time also get a reminder at that time.

Backups

Download a file with all of your homework, grades and settings to keep somewhere safe. The server also saves a copy of everyone's data once a day.

Download my backup

    Restore

    Put your data back from a backup file (one you downloaded, or the server's daily copy). Only your own homework, grades and settings are replaced; a safety copy is saved first and your password is kept.

    Account

    Your password. Each device asks for it once, then remembers for a year.

    People

    Everyone with an account. New people join from the sign-in page with the invite code; you can also add someone here, reset a forgotten password, or remove an account (that deletes their data).
